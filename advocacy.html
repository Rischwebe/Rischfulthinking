Advocacy

WHO HAVE WE DECIDED
MATTERS LESS?

It is not a campaign slogan. It is the question underneath a great deal of what I care about.

PEOPLE ARE NOT SYSTEM PROBLEMS.

I understand disability as an autistic adult and as a mother raising children with significant special needs. I have watched people be underestimated and families blamed when inaccessible systems fail them.

Dignity should not depend on being convenient, typical, wealthy, easy to understand or useful to somebody in power.

THE WORK CONNECTS.

Disability & neurodivergence

Autism, access, special-needs families and systems designed around actual human beings.

Family, adoption & race

International adoption, multiracial families, anti-racism and honest attention to identity and power.

Human dignity

LGBTQ+ and trans rights, women, survivors, children, animals and people institutions find easy to ignore.

Tell me somebody is being treated unfairly?
Now I’m listening.

Compassion is not gossip. Caring about a person does not require knowing their private drama. It requires recognizing that their life matters.
