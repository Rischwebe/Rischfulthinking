The Mystic

I BELIEVE IN GHOSTS.
I ALSO BELIEVE IN CHECKING THE SOURCE.

THREE
GENERATIONS.

03

My grandmother saw spirits. My mother saw spirits. I see spirits.

My grandmother was known as the Witch of Antigo in Antigo, Wisconsin. I was literally raised as a witch—not merely “around spirituality.” Spirits, intuition, psychic experiences and witchcraft were part of my childhood worldview.

Other families passed down recipes. Mine passed down dead people.

SUPPLEMENTAL READING.

I read Shirley MacLaine’s Out on a Limb around third grade. It did not begin my spiritual life; apparently I was doing supplemental reading.

Across decades of practicing and developing mediumship, my influences and work or training have included Sylvia Browne, John Holland and Sonia Choquette.

THE RIGHT TO SAY
“I DON’T KNOW.”

I have had experiences I cannot explain and known things I had no normal way of knowing. I believe those experiences happened. That does not require me to pretend I can explain the mechanics.

Intuition can coexist with confirmation bias. A sincere psychic can be mistaken. A confident psychic can be completely full of shit. Every coincidence is not cosmic mail, and every flickering bulb is not Grandma. Sometimes you need a new bulb.

“A spirit told me” is not a research citation.

Mystery is allowed.
Bullshit is optional.
