PRIVACY.

This site does not currently collect contact submissions or run first-party analytics.

Information collected

Rischful Thinking is currently a static website. Its contact form is visibly disabled and does not submit information. No newsletter, account system or comments feature is operating.

Analytics and cookies

No site analytics have been configured and this site does not intentionally set its own tracking cookies. This notice will be updated before analytics or similar tools are added.

External services

Links to YouTube and Threads take you to external services governed by their own privacy practices. The site currently links to YouTube rather than embedding a player. If embeds are added later, YouTube may receive technical information when an embedded video loads, and this policy will be updated.

Future contact submissions

If a contact service is enabled later, this page will identify it and explain what is collected, why, and how long it is retained before submissions are accepted.
